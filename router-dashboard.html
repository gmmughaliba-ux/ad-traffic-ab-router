    What am I looking at? (plain-English guide)

    Traffic split: your ads send everyone to the router, which randomly sends each new visitor to one of your sites. This shows whether the split is roughly even. If a bar sits far outside its band, something's off (a site may be down, or misconfigured).

    Health banner: green = all good; yellow = worth a look (a site drifting, or low numbers); red = a real problem (no fresh data, or a site getting zero visitors).

    This is a quick daily glance. Your real experiment analysis (which site converts best) lives in GA4 — this dashboard just confirms the plumbing is healthy.

    Auto-refreshes every 60 seconds when connected to a live feed. Counts exclude bots.
